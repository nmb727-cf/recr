# User Personas — Recruitment Platform

## Document Purpose
This document defines core user types, behaviors, pain points and motivations.
All agents must reference this before building any feature.

---

## USER TYPE 1: RECRUITMENT AGENCY

### Overview
Agencies connect companies with candidates and manage entire recruitment lifecycle.
Most active daily users of the platform. Size ranges from 2 to 50+ people.

### Size Segments
- Micro: 2-5 people (owner plus 1-4 recruiters)
- Small: 5-20 people
- Medium: 20-50 people
- Large: 50+ people

### Typical Daily Workflow
1. Client sends job requirement via email to owner or coordinator
2. Owner extracts JD and passes to recruiter or sourcer
3. Recruiter sources candidates from LinkedIn, job boards, references, internal DB
4. Recruiter connects via call, email or WhatsApp
5. Discusses opportunity, checks interest and availability
6. Submits interested candidate profile to company
7. Coordinates interview scheduling between company and candidate
8. Manages feedback, follow-ups, offer negotiations
9. Closes position and invoices client

### Current Tools and Pain Points
- Excel sheets for candidate tracking
- WhatsApp for all communication
- Email for JDs and CV submissions
- Free or basic ATS not integrated with anything
- No CRM for client management
- 40-60% of working time on data entry not recruiting

### Biggest Frustrations
- Too much data entry not enough actual recruiting
- No single system connecting candidates, clients and jobs
- Losing candidate data when recruiters leave
- No team pipeline visibility
- Duplicate candidates added by multiple recruiters
- No automated follow-up system
- Manual interview coordination wastes hours daily

### What Would Make Them Switch Immediately
- Chain effect where one action triggers next automatically
- Deep networking connecting all parties
- Minimal data entry and fast data processing
- Candidate database that stays with agency forever
- Automated coordination between company and candidate
- Mobile friendly since recruiters work on the go

## USER TYPE 2: COMPANY / EMPLOYER

### Overview
Companies range from 20 to 20,000+ employees. They are paying clients of agencies
and direct users of the platform ATS features.

### Size Segments
- Small: 20-100 employees, HR handles everything alone
- Medium: 100-500 employees, dedicated HR team
- Large: 500-5000 employees, HR plus hiring managers plus department heads
- Enterprise: 5000+ employees, branch wise HR plus regional agencies plus central HR

### Who Handles Recruitment
- Small: Single HR person handles everything
- Medium: HR team with hiring manager involvement
- Large: Dedicated talent acquisition team plus hiring managers
- Enterprise: Branch wise HR teams plus regional agencies plus central HR oversight

### Typical Workflow
1. Hiring manager raises requirement to HR
2. HR creates JD and gets requisition approval
3. JD shared with empanelled agencies via email
4. Agencies submit CVs via email or basic submission portal
5. HR screens CVs manually which is very time consuming
6. Shortlisted candidates scheduled for interviews
7. Multiple interview rounds coordinated manually
8. Offer letter generation and approval
9. Joining formalities and onboarding

### Current Tools and Pain Points
- CVs from multiple agencies arriving via email creating inbox chaos
- No unified view of all candidates across all agencies
- Duplicate candidates submitted by multiple agencies causing ownership disputes
- Manual CV screening takes days
- Interview scheduling done via email and phone
- No visibility into agency performance
- Tracking offer status manually across multiple candidates

### What Our Platform Provides
- Full ATS with pipeline management
- Agency management to empanel, manage and track all vendors in one place
- Automated CV screening with AI threshold marking
- Auto shortlisting and rejection based on criteria
- All interview types built in:
  - AI screening interviews
  - Video interviews
  - Panel interviews
  - Technical assessments
  - Group discussions
- Interview scheduling with calendar integration
- Hiring hierarchy with multi-level approval workflows
- Approval hierarchy for offers and requisitions
- Non-discreet hiring support for confidential positions
- Branch wise hiring management for large enterprises
- Agency performance analytics
- Candidate ownership tracking to prevent duplicate disputes

## USER TYPE 3: CANDIDATE

### Overview
Candidates are the most underserved users in recruitment technology today.
They do the most repetitive work yet have the least control and visibility.
Our platform gives candidates unprecedented control through the Talent Passport.

### Current Candidate Journey and Pain Points
1. Searches job boards and LinkedIn for opportunities
2. Creates profile on each platform separately
3. Uploads CV to every platform individually
4. Fills same personal details form repeatedly
5. Receives agency calls and sends CV via email every single time
6. Interview scheduling forces them to take time off work
7. Travels to company office for every interview round
8. No visibility into where their CV has gone
9. No control over who has their data
10. No way to revoke data from companies they no longer want

### Biggest Frustrations
- Filling same forms repeatedly across platforms
- Sending CV via email every time an agency calls
- Taking time off work for daytime interviews
- Traveling to different cities just for first round interviews
- No idea who has their profile data
- No single place showing complete professional story
- Interview results not portable so proving skills repeatedly

## TALENT PASSPORT — Core Differentiator

### Concept
A global professional identity like a credit report but for your career.
One link. Complete professional profile. Candidate controlled.

### What the Passport Contains
- Professional details always visible cannot be hidden
- Resume or CV latest version always current
- Skill assessments and exam credentials
- Mock interview results and scores
- Profile heat score showing how in-demand this candidate is
- Interview history and performance
- Certifications and verifications
- Work history verification
- References

### How Sharing Works
- Candidate gets a unique shareable link
- Sends link instead of CV to any agency or company
- If recipient uses our platform one click imports all data to their system
- If recipient does not use platform they see a professional profile page
- Candidate chooses what is visible and what is hidden
- Professional details always visible and cannot be hidden
- Personal details like address and phone are candidate controlled

### Data Control Panel
Candidate has a dashboard showing:
- Every company and agency that has accessed their passport
- When they accessed it and what data they viewed
- Option to REVOKE access at any time
- Notification when someone new views their profile
- Ability to update data and all connected parties see updated version immediately

## APPLICATION TRACKING — Critical Feature

Every candidate sees their application status in real time:

- Applied
- CV Received by Agency or Company
- CV Under Review
- Shortlisted or Rejected with reason
- Interview Scheduled with date, time and format
- Interview Completed
- Feedback Pending
- Second Round or Offer or Rejected
- Offer Released
- Offer Accepted or Declined
- Joining Confirmed

No more silence after applying.
No more wondering if CV was even received.
Every action by agency or company is visible to candidate in real time.

## 24-48 HOUR ACTION TRIGGER SYSTEM

Every action in the system has a deadline:

- CV review after submission: 24 hours, if not done auto reminder to HR
- Interview scheduling after shortlist: 48 hours, if not done escalate to manager
- Interview feedback after interview: 24 hours, if not done auto reminder to interviewer
- Offer release after final round: 48 hours, if not done escalate to hiring manager
- Candidate response to offer: 48 hours, if not done auto reminder to candidate
- Agency follow up after submission: 24 hours, if not done auto reminder to recruiter

Every task gets automatic deadline.
System sends reminder at 50% of deadline.
If no action at deadline escalates to next person in hierarchy.
Full audit trail of all escalations.
Analytics show which stage has most delays.

## REGIONAL CONFIGURATION NOTE

Regional differences are NOT separate features.
The platform has ONE feature set that is configurable per region or preference.
Examples:
- WhatsApp integration available globally, configurable per region
- GDPR controls available globally, mandatory where required by law
- Data privacy levels configurable per company or country
- Language support configurable per user preference

Agents must NEVER build region specific code.
Build globally. Configure locally.

## KEY INSIGHTS FOR ALL AGENTS

When building any feature always ask:
- Does this reduce data entry for any user?
- Does this give the candidate MORE control not less?
- Does this work on mobile with slow internet?
- Does this respect data privacy by default?
- Does this work across time zones for global users?
- Does the candidate own their data or does the platform?
- Does this action have a 24-48 hour trigger attached?
- Is this configurable globally not hardcoded for one region?
- Does this reduce the recruitment cycle time?
