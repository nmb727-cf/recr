# User Guide: Hiring Decision Command Center (HDC)

Welcome to the **Hiring Decision Command Center**. This guide will help you navigate the post-interview hiring lifecycle, from making the final "Hire" decision to tracking a candidate's first day.

---

## 1. What is Hiring Decision Center?
The HDC is a unified workspace for recruiters, hiring managers, and leadership to collaborate on final hiring choices. It replaces messy email threads and spreadsheets with a structured, audited workflow for approvals, compensation modeling, and offer management.

## 2. When to use HDC?
Use HDC as soon as a candidate has completed their final interview round. It is the "Command Center" for everything that happens between **Interview Completion** and **Joining Date**.

## 3. Hiring Decision Flow Overview
The HDC follows a logical, step-by-step path:
**Committee Review** → **Executive Approval** → **Offer Modeling** → **Negotiation** → **Offer Release** → **Acceptance** → **Joining Tracking**

---

## 4. Decision Dashboard
*   **What it's for:** A high-level view of all active hiring decisions across the organization.
*   **When to use it:** Daily, to track bottlenecks and pending actions.
*   **Who should use it:** Recruiters and Talent Acquisition Leads.
*   **Actions:** View pending votes, see offers awaiting release, and track upcoming joining dates.

## 5. Hiring Committee
*   **What it's for:** Gathering structured feedback and votes from multiple stakeholders.
*   **When to use it:** When a collaborative decision is needed for a key role.
*   **Who should use it:** Interviewers, Hiring Managers, and Peer Reviewers.
*   **Actions:** Submit a "Hire", "Reject", or "Hold" vote with supporting notes.

## 6. Candidate Comparison
*   **What it's for:** Side-by-side comparison of finalists for a single position.
*   **When to use it:** When you have 2-3 great candidates but only 1 headcount.
*   **Who should use it:** Hiring Managers and Recruiters.
*   **Actions:** Compare scores, ranks, and technical evaluation results.

## 7. Compensation Planning (Offer Intelligence)
*   **What it's for:** Modeling different salary and benefit scenarios.
*   **When to use it:** Before an offer is extended, to ensure internal equity and market competitiveness.
*   **Who should use it:** Recruiters and HR/Comp & Ben teams.
*   **Actions:** Create "Scenarios" (e.g., Base-heavy vs. Bonus-heavy) and select the recommended one.

## 8. Negotiation
*   **What it's for:** Tracking counter-offers and candidate expectations.
*   **When to use it:** If a candidate requests changes to the initial offer.
*   **Who should use it:** Recruiters.
*   **Actions:** Log candidate asks, submit company counter-offers, and mark as "Agreed".

## 9. Offer Release
*   **What it's for:** The final step of sending the formal offer letter.
*   **When to use it:** Once approval is granted and compensation is agreed upon.
*   **Who should use it:** Recruiters or HR Managers.
*   **Actions:** Freeze offer details, generate the letter, and "Release" to candidate.

## 10. Joining Tracking
*   **What it's for:** Monitoring the "Pre-boarding" period.
*   **When to use it:** After a candidate accepts the offer, until their first day.
*   **Who should use it:** HR Operations and Hiring Managers.
*   **Actions:** Confirm actual joining date and hand off to onboarding teams.

---

## Role-Based Quick Guides

### 📘 Recruiter Guide
Your goal is to keep the momentum. Use the **Dashboard** to find stuck approvals and the **Negotiation** tab to bridge the gap between candidate asks and company budgets.

### 📗 Hiring Manager Guide
Focus on the **Committee** and **Approval** tabs. Your main action is to review the feedback from your team and provide the final "Go" or "No-Go" for the offer.

### 📙 Leadership Guide
Use the **Decision Dashboard** for executive oversight. Focus on high-level metrics like offer acceptance rates and average time-to-hire in the HDC phase.

### 📕 Admin Guide
Configure **Governance Rules** (who can approve what) and monitor **Audit Logs** for compliance. Ensure all roles are correctly assigned to their respective departments.
